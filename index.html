What these numbers mean

Ages 15 and older: daily averages are based on the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics’ 2025 American Time Use Survey, the latest release as of October 2026. It was published June 25, 2026. The averages include weekdays, weekends, workers and nonworkers.

Two adult categories are derived: sleep is personal care minus 0.77 hours of non-sleep care. Travel is modeled from work and school time using the 2024 ACS mean commute, plus a 0.5-hour non-commute baseline. “Other” is the residual and includes shopping, reading, relaxing, calls, volunteering, religion and uncategorized leisure.

Under 15: these are explicitly estimates, not ATUS measurements. They combine pediatric sleep guidance, school-calendar averages, media-use research and physical-activity guidance. They are best read as age-appropriate portraits, not precise national diary results.
